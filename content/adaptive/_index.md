*Adaptive* is a practical framework for organizations navigating workforce evolution — from reactive scheduling to adaptive, intelligent systems. Part I lays the foundations: the Collaborative Intelligence Era, the history of the discipline, the ten change drivers, the operating frameworks, and the case for variance over the plan. Part II is a five-level playbook — the WFM Labs Maturity Model — that takes an operation from an Excel foundation to enterprise orchestration.

The whole book is here to read and to listen to, chapter by chapter, with the audio following you as you move through the text. Each chapter links to its concept page on the [WFM Labs wiki](https://wiki.wfmlabs.org/wiki/Adaptive_Concepts), and *Ask the Book* answers questions from the text itself and takes you to the passage.
